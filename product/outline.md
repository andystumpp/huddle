# Huddle

> An AI teammate that quietly watches over your shoulder and offers genuinely useful, unsolicited advice — like a good kibitzer, but for your work.

---

## North Star

**Huddle turns what you're already doing on your screen into a stream of small, well-timed insights — surfacing things to write about, ways to work faster, and patterns you'd never notice yourself — without you having to ask or log anything.**

The measure of success is simple: *did Huddle tell me something today that I'm glad I knew, that I wouldn't have thought of on my own?* Everything in the design below serves that one question. If a feature doesn't make the suggestions sharper, better-timed, or more trustworthy, it doesn't belong in Huddle.

---

## What It Is

Huddle is a Windows desktop app that follows along with what you're doing on your screen, understands the context, and delivers nudges (plus a running history) tuned to scenarios *you* configure — starting with **social media ideas** and **efficiency coaching**.

It's well-integrated into the desktop — sitting slightly in the background, out of your way, but always there the moment you want to look. Not a webpage you open, not a window you have to manage. Just *there*.

It is **not** a surveillance tool, a time tracker, or a screen recorder. It captures to understand, not to archive.

## Goals

- Surface high-quality, scenario-specific suggestions from real screen activity.
- Be useful without being noisy or annoying.
- Make "scenarios" (social media ideas, efficiency, anything else) configurable prompt-driven lenses, not hard-coded features.
- Feel like a natural part of the desktop — quietly in the background, instantly there when you want it, with low overhead.
